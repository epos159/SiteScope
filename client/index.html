SiteScope: pre-construction site research for Pennsylvania

Know the site before you bid the job.

SiteScope pulls the public data a contractor, engineer, or permit coordinator checks before bidding or designing a project into a single report. Search any address in a supported county and see the parcel on a satellite map with everything below in one place.

What a SiteScope report includes

  • Parcel records from county GIS: boundary, parcel ID, owner, acreage, and adjoining parcels.
  • FEMA flood zones from the National Flood Hazard Layer, including floodway and base flood elevation where mapped.
  • Soils from the USDA NRCS soil survey (SSURGO): soil type, slope class, drainage, hydrologic group, and hydric rating.
  • Topography from USGS 3DEP elevation data: elevation range and slope across the parcel.
  • Wetlands from the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service National Wetlands Inventory.

Exports

Download the report as PDF, PNG, or CSV, or export a DXF drawing for AutoCAD, Civil 3D, and other CAD software. The DXF uses PA State Plane South coordinates and includes the parcel boundary with lengths, adjoining parcels, flood zones, and wetlands on separate layers.
