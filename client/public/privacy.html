Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

SiteScope (sitescope.poschventures.com) is a pre-construction site research tool operated by Posch Ventures (“we,” “us”). This policy explains what information we collect when you use SiteScope, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you create an account, we receive your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and profile photo. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. If you use an email and password, your password is stored and verified by Firebase — we never see it.
  • Searches. The addresses and map locations you look up are sent to our server so we can retrieve public records for that site. We do not keep a search history tied to you; results are held only briefly in temporary memory to make repeat lookups faster.
  • Free searches. Visitors without an account are given a random anonymous ID so we can count their free searches. For each free search we store that ID with the approximate location searched. This lets you reopen those places for free, and keeps the trial from being reset. To limit abuse, we also keep a daily count of free searches per network. The count is stored under a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, never the address itself. If you create an account, your anonymous ID becomes your account, so searches you already used carry over.
  • Usage analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how SiteScope is used — for example, pages viewed, searches run, export formats chosen, and sign-ins. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your browser, device type, and approximate location. When you are signed in, an anonymous account identifier is attached so we can count active users; your name and email are not sent to Google Analytics.
  • Server logs. Our hosting providers keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, requested URL, and time) for security and troubleshooting.
  • Payment information. If you subscribe, Stripe processes the payment and stores your card. We receive your subscription status and the billing details Stripe shares with us, such as name, email, and billing address. We do not store your card number.

Where site data comes from

To build a site report, our server sends the location you searched to public data services, including county GIS parcel services, FEMA, the USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service, the U.S. Geological Survey, the U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service, the U.S. Census Bureau, and OpenStreetMap. Only the address or coordinates needed for the lookup are sent — never your account information.

How we use information

  • To provide SiteScope, including searches, reports, and exports.
  • To manage accounts, the free search limit, and subscriptions.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.
  • To understand which features are useful and improve SiteScope.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes to the service.

What we don’t do

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We share information only with the service providers that run SiteScope — Google (Firebase Authentication, Firestore, and Analytics), Stripe (payments), Render (application server), and Cloudflare (website hosting) — or when required by law.

Your choices

  • Delete your account. Email support@poschventures.com and we will delete your account and associated information.
  • Clear local data. Clearing your browser’s site data removes the sign-in session stored on your device. Free searches already used are still counted on our server.
  • Opt out of analytics. You can block Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or your browser’s tracking-protection settings.

Data security and retention

All traffic to SiteScope is encrypted (HTTPS). Account data is kept while your account is active and deleted on request. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics settings.

Children

SiteScope is a professional tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information — for example, if we add paid plans — we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@poschventures.com.
